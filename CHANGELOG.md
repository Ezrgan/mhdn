# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First usable overlay.

Floating damage numbers stay on the world point of the hit and follow the camera.
Color and size come from the last 50 hits (white, light yellow, orange); poison
ticks stay small and purple. A corner recount shows hunt damage and DPS. The
menu bar reports whether Azahar, the RPC server, and the game build are ready.
Leaving a quest, rewinding a frame, or loading a save state clears the numbers.

Hits that share a guest frame still merge when the damage tap is not installed.
Crits, element, and which hunter swung are not known yet.
